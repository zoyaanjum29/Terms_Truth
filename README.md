# TermsTruth – Understand Before You Agree

### An AI-powered platform that helps people understand what they are agreeing to before accepting online terms and conditions.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://terms-truth.vercel.app

---

## About the Project

TermsTruth is an AI-powered platform designed to help users understand lengthy Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policies, and online agreements in simple language.

Every day, people accept online agreements without reading them because legal documents are often lengthy, complicated, and difficult to understand.

These agreements may contain important conditions related to personal data, subscription renewals, refunds, content usage permissions, and user rights.

TermsTruth addresses this problem by identifying important clauses, explaining their potential real-world impact, and displaying the original agreement text as evidence.

Our goal is to make online agreements easier to understand and help users make more informed decisions before accepting them.

## Problem Statement

Most websites and applications require users to accept Terms and Conditions before accessing their services. However, users often skip reading these agreements because of their length and complex legal language.

As a result, they may overlook important conditions that could affect their privacy, payments, content, and usage rights.

TermsTruth helps users identify and understand these conditions without having to interpret lengthy legal documents themselves.

## Key Features

### 1. AI-Powered Agreement Analysis
Analyzes Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policies to identify important clauses using Google Gemini AI.

### 2. Attention-Level Classification
Categorizes identified findings into three levels:

- **Important:** Clauses that deserve particular attention.
- **Be Aware:** Conditions users should understand.
- **Informational:** Other useful agreement details.

### 3. Real-World Impact Explanations
Explains how specific clauses could affect users in everyday situations using simple language.

### 4. Actionable Suggestions
Provides practical points users can consider or check before agreeing.

### 5. Original Clause Evidence
Displays the relevant original agreement text so users can verify the AI-generated explanations.

### 6. Chrome Browser Extension
Allows users to analyze Terms and Conditions directly from webpages without manually copying the entire agreement.

### 7. Web Dashboard
Provides a platform to paste or upload agreements, view detailed analysis, and access previous analyses through history.

## AI Integration – Google Gemini

TermsTruth integrates **Google Gemini AI** to make complex legal language easier to understand.

Gemini powers the agreement analysis process by helping the platform:

- Identify important clauses from lengthy agreements.
- Interpret complex legal language.
- Generate simple and understandable explanations.
- Explain the potential real-world impact of specific conditions.
- Provide practical suggestions for users.

By combining AI-powered analysis with original clause evidence, TermsTruth aims to make agreement analysis more understandable and transparent.

## How It Works

1. Open the TermsTruth website or Chrome extension.
2. Paste or upload an agreement on the website, or open a webpage containing Terms and Conditions.
3. Start the analysis.
4. The AI analyzes the agreement and identifies important clauses.
5. Review the attention levels, explanations, and practical suggestions.
6. Expand the original clause to verify the finding.

## Chrome Extension Installation

TermsTruth also provides a Chrome browser extension for analyzing agreements directly from webpages.

To install it:

1. Download this repository as a ZIP file.
2. Extract the downloaded folder.
3. Open Google Chrome.
4. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
5. Enable **Developer mode**.
6. Click **Load unpacked**.
7. Select the `extension` folder from the extracted project.
8. Pin TermsTruth to your Chrome toolbar.

The extension is now ready to use.
